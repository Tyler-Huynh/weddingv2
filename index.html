How It Began

Our Story

We are high school sweethearts! We went to the same middle school and high school, but did not meet until Maggie's junior year and Tyler's senior year. In October of 2018, our paths crossed when we were both volunteer counselors at Outdoor Lab in Windy Peak.

Spending a full week in the mountains sparked our friendship, and we started dating soon after! Our journey has been nothing but excitement and love since then.

After we both graduated from high school, Tyler first and then Maggie, we both made our way to the University of Colorado Boulder, where we studied Computer Science! Being in college together was so fun and filled with lots of friends and, of course, lots of studying.

After both graduating, we started our lives together, and our relationship took the next step when Tyler proposed to Maggie at Twin Falls during a trip to Seattle.

We are so excited to start this next chapter of our lives and cannot wait to celebrate with all of our favorite people <3
