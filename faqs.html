Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the RSVP deadline?

We ask that all guests RSVP by February 15, 2027, so we have ample time to plan.

What should I wear?

Our dress code is Black-Tie Preferred. We ask that men wear a tuxedo or dark-colored suit and tie. For women, floor-length evening gowns are encouraged, but a mid-length dress is also appropriate.

Will the wedding be indoors or outdoors?

Our ceremony will be held outdoors and then we will transition inside for the reception. Colorado weather can be very unpredictable, so please dress accordingly and be prepared.

Is there parking on site?

Yes, there is a large parking lot at the front of the venue.

What kind of food will be served?

We will be serving a mixture of Chinese and Vietnamese family-style dishes. We hope you enjoy it.
